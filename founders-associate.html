We're an AI-native consulting and procurement solutions firm, unlocking value for mid-sized European industrial companies. We address opportunities across the entire supplier relationship, from supplier spend analysis down to supplier negotiations, developing our proprietary tools and solutions at each step of the way.

If growing a company from 0 to 1, working closely with European industrial champions, and learning about AI and procurement resonate with you, Ordito is a great fit.

01 · Where we stand
  • Less than three months in.
  • Deep industry expertise, through our founders' experience.
  • First clients signed.
  • Everything else to be built, and too much work for our four hands to handle!
02 · That's why we need you

You will work closely with our founders, Patrick & Cedrick, across all dimensions of the business.

AWhat you'll do
Primary focus

AI & automation, to make our day-to-day project execution work more efficient

  • Mapping our workflow and identifying automation opportunities.
  • Developing AI-enabled workflows and analytics tools.
  • Experiment freely: we'd rather you try ten things and ship five than wait for instructions.

And you will naturally get broader exposure to other activities:

  • Project execution: contribution to client-facing execution tasks, including market and supplier research, spend data analytics and production of deliverables.
  • Commercial development: identification of prospects and preparation of prospection materials.

We know this may sound broad, and we understand you cannot and should not do it all either. The reality is, you'll focus on what you're most passionate about and where you believe you can bring most value.

BWhat we're looking for

We're looking for someone:

  • Curious and motivated, with a strong interest in AI and a genuine appetite to learn.
  • Who can bring new ideas, think critically and with good judgement.
  • With at least 6 months of prior work experience.
  • Comfortable working in English.

Not a clue about what procurement is? That's normal, and we'll cover that part. Some coding experience appreciated, but not indispensable.

Don't tick every box but recognise yourself in this? Apply anyway. We're not recruiting a CV.

03 · What we have to offer
  • A front-row seat as our company goes from zero to one: it'll just be you, Patrick and Cedrick. Until our next hire 😉
  • The chance to learn from, and together with, an A-team.
  • A full-time position if we all realise we want to continue working together.
  • EUR 3,000 a month, a relocation bonus if you are not yet based in Brussels, and anything you deem necessary to do your job well. We will spare no expense on your development and well-being.
04 · Process

In English. Under two weeks from first contact to a decision.

  1. Send your CV and tell us about the most interesting thing you have built or worked on. No cover letter needed.
  2. A 30-minute call.
  3. A short exercise. No consulting case interview.
  4. An hour with both of us.
  5. A decision. Every application gets an answer.
