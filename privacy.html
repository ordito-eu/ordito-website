Ordito respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit ordito.eu or contact us, why we process it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What personal data we process, and why

When you contact us

If you reach out to us by email or phone, we process the data you provide: your name, email address, telephone number, company, and the content of your message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and to follow up on a potential business relationship. Legal basis: taking steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Website hosting

Our website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). When you visit the site, GitHub automatically receives technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, which it uses to deliver the website and protect it against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google's servers, which involves the transmission of your IP address to Google LLC. We use this service to display the site consistently across devices. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies. If you switch the site language, your choice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage); this information stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run our website and communications:

  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (GitHub Pages)
  • Google LLC — web fonts
  • Microsoft Corporation — business email and document infrastructure (Microsoft 365)

These providers are established in the United States. Transfers of personal data to them are safeguarded by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep contact correspondence only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and we delete it when it is no longer needed, unless a legal obligation requires longer storage. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider for a limited period for security purposes.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data rectified
  • have your data erased
  • restrict the processing of your data
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@ordito.eu. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Belgium, this is the Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels — www.dataprotectionauthority.be.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when our website or services change. The current version, with its date of last revision, is always available on this page.
